# The built-in hordes

Five hordes ship with kowalski. Each is a folder of Markdown you can read; your own copy with the
same id replaces the built-in one.

| Horde | You give it | You get |
|---|---|---|
| **Spreadsheet analyst** | questions about your workbooks in tableski | `report.xlsx` (one sheet per question) and short answers. The model writes SQL; every number comes from the query engine. **Ask more about this data** asks a follow-up on the same tables. |
| **Morning brief** | the pages you follow and what you care about | `BRIEF.md`: a top pick and the items worth your time from each page, with links. A weekday 7:00 schedule is ready to switch on. Saves to your notes vault. |
| **Folder watcher** | documents dropped into its `inbox/` | a note per document: what it is, the key facts, what to do by when. Watches its inbox once switched on. |
| **Knowledge compiler** | a question and the pages that should answer it | one note answering the question, with its sources. Saves to your notes vault. |
| **URL summarizer** | a list of links | a short summary per page. |

The Spreadsheet analyst needs tableski connected. Scheduled and watching hordes ship switched off.
